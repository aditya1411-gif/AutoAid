Basic Travel Essentials

Make sure your car and you are road-ready! Here’s a checklist of must-have items every traveler should carry.

⚙️ Car Maintenance Essentials

  • ✔ Spare tire & jack
  • ✔ Jumper cables
  • ✔ Tool kit
  • ✔ Engine oil & coolant
  • ✔ Tire pressure gauge

🧳 Personal & Safety Essentials

  • ✔ First aid kit
  • ✔ Emergency contact list
  • ✔ Flashlight & batteries
  • ✔ Blanket & raincoat
  • ✔ Power bank

🍎 Comfort & Travel Aids

  • ✔ Water bottles & snacks
  • ✔ Car phone mount
  • ✔ Maps or GPS
  • ✔ Sunglasses
  • ✔ Travel pillow
